# Design archive
<!-- раздел: Архив КД | инструмент: archive | pro -->
## What it is for

Answers from the archive of design documentation with links to the files: «what material is side member 714094 made of», «where was 09G2S sheet used after 2020».

## How to start

Menu **Design → «Design archive…»**. Once the archive is indexed, the chat searches it together with the project by itself: the source in the answer is marked «Архив КД: …», the file opens on a click. It is switched on and off with **+** at the input field → «Design archive». A question with a designation (e.g. 792720.2801214) or the words «drawing», «specification» always goes to the archive. The agent searches the archive too (the «search the design archive» tool). A question to the archive only — `/архив question`.

## Step by step

1. «Add folder…» — the folders of the archive.
2. «Recognise scans» — with the built-in recognition of Windows (a Russian language pack is needed for Russian scans).
3. **«Update index»**. The first time 30 thousand files take about an hour; it can be stopped and continued later.
4. After that «Update index» reads only new and changed files — minutes.
5. In the chat: `/архив материал поперечины 714094`.

## What you get

«Sources» under the answer; a click opens the file. Read are KOMPAS-3D files (designation, material, mass, «Разработал» (designer), date, contents) without starting KOMPAS, PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, old .doc and .xls, DXF and scans.

## If it does not work

- The product number (the folder name) and years in the question narrow the search: «в 2016», «после 2019», «2016–2018».
- The search is by words: ask with the words of the documents — «лонжерон», «09Г2С», a designation.
- DWG files are read when ODA File Converter is installed. The technical requirements on the field of a KOMPAS drawing are seen only in PDF copies.
