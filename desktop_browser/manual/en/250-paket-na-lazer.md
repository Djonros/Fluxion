# Laser package
<!-- раздел: КОМПАС-3D | инструмент: laser | pro -->
## What it is for

Flat patterns of all sheet parts of an assembly in DXF 1:1, without a frame and texts, sorted into folders by thickness.

## How to start

Menu **Tools → «Laser package (DXFka)…»**.

## Step by step

1. Choose the `.a3d` assembly.
2. Choose a folder for the DXF: a folder named after the assembly is made in it, with subfolders by thickness in mm.
3. The progress is in the chat. If the thickness cannot be found, Fluxion asks.

## What you get

Parts with a sheet body and with a material «Лист …» (sheet), including subassemblies, executions and local parts. At the end — how many DXF per thickness and a link to the folder. When the sheet body and the material disagree, the thickness of the material is taken.

## If it does not work

- A manual step: check the view in KOMPAS and press «Continue», «Skip part» or «Stop».
