# Voice and hands-free talk
<!-- раздел: Чат и документы | инструмент: - -->
## What it is for

To dictate questions, listen to the answers and talk by voice without touching the keyboard.

## How to start

The buttons on the right of the input field: **microphone** (Ctrl+Space) and **headphones** (Ctrl+Shift+Space); reading answers aloud — **+** → «Read answers aloud».

## Step by step

1. The first time you press the microphone, Fluxion offers to download the speech model (Whisper Small, 484 MB — good on an ordinary processor) and starts listening as soon as it is downloaded. Another model — menu «Models» (Ctrl+M).
2. **Microphone** — speak; the recording stops by itself after a pause. The text appears in the input field.
3. **+** → **«Read answers aloud»** — answers are read aloud with a Windows voice. Every answer has «Read aloud» under it.
4. **Headphones** — hands-free talk: Fluxion listens, sends, reads the answer and listens again. It is turned off by the word **«Stop»** («Стоп»).
5. «Компас, втулка 30 на 20 длиной 50» said aloud becomes the command `/компас …`.

## What you get

Speech is recognised on the computer; the sound is not sent anywhere.

## If it does not work

- No voice for reading in your language: Windows Settings → Time & language → Speech — add the language.
- To send right after dictation: the chat settings button (the gear) → «Send right after dictation».
