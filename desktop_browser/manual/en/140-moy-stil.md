# My writing style
<!-- раздел: Чат и документы | инструмент: writing_style | pro -->
## What it is for

Memos, explanatory notes and letters to customers — in your own manner: forms of address, turns of phrase, signature. Without fine-tuning and without a graphics card.

## How to start

Menu **Tools → «My writing style…»**, then the **«✍ My style»** chip under the input field.

## Step by step

1. «Add files…» or «Paste text…» — 3–10 of your own texts, best a few of each kind.
2. Check the «Kind of text» column.
3. **«Describe the style»** — the model reads the samples (up to a couple of minutes).
4. Correct the description if needed and «Save».
5. Turn on «✍ My style» and write: «a memo to buy 09G2S sheet 10 mm, 1.8 t, by 5 November».

## What you get

A text in your manner. Fluxion takes the facts only from the request and leaves what is missing [in brackets].

## If it does not work

- You added new samples — «Describe the style» and «Save» again.
- For ordinary questions turn «✍ My style» off: the answers are shorter.
