# Materials catalog
<!-- раздел: КОМПАС-3D | инструмент: materials -->
## What it is for

Your own materials and sheets with their range of thicknesses, for the parts Fluxion builds.

## How to start

Menu **Design → «Materials catalog…»**.

## Step by step

1. Open the catalog and add materials or import them from Excel/CSV.
2. Save — the new materials are available in «Part in KOMPAS» at once.

## What you get

Fluxion recognises your materials in the descriptions of parts and sets their density.

## If it does not work

- Check that the import file has the name and the density.
