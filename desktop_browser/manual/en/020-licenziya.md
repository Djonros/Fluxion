## What it is for

Pro opens the engineering tools (assembly reports, the cost estimate, the purchase list, standards checks, the laser package, the design archive), «My writing style», the agent that edits files, fine-tuning, large and cloud models.

## How to start

Menu **Help → «License…»** or the **FREE/PRO** badge at the bottom right.

## Step by step

1. To buy: the **«Buy Pro…»** button in the licence window or the «Prices» section on the site.
2. The key comes as a `.key` file.
3. **«Load key file…»** or drag the file into the licence window. You can also paste the whole text of the e-mail.

## What you get

Pro turns on at once, without a restart and without the internet: the key is checked on your computer.

## If it does not work

- The licence window shows up to which date the key covers new versions. A newer version without a renewal works as the free one.
- The key is not accepted — write to the address in the «Prices» section of the site and attach the key file.
