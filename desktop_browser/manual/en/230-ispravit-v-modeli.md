# Fix in the model and Set material
<!-- раздел: КОМПАС-3D | инструмент: assembly_report | pro -->
## What it is for

To fix typical errors of an assembly right in the KOMPAS model, with your consent and with a backup copy.

## How to start

The buttons in the «Before the report» window: **«Fix in the model (N)…»** and **«Set material (N)…»**.

## Step by step

1. **Close the assembly and the parts in KOMPAS-3D** — an open model is not changed.
2. «Fix in the model» moves bought items to «Other items» («Прочие изделия») of the specification of their assemblies.
3. «Set material» — mark the parts, press «Choose in the KOMPAS library…» and choose a material or a sortament.
4. Fluxion shows the list of fixes and the files that will change, and asks for your consent.

## What you get

Before the fix every file is copied to the **«Резервные копии Fluxion»** (Fluxion backups) folder next to it. After the fix the report is made again.

## If it does not work

- To roll back: take the file back from «Резервные копии Fluxion».
- The files of bought items and shared libraries are not changed. If a part lies outside the product folder, Fluxion warns you.
